# 2026.314.1533 — refactor(schema): derive the cdp word sets from vocabulary tables

## Summary

Convert the two inline `|` word unions in `candy/plugin-cdp/schema/cdp.cue` to the §10.1 S3
vocabulary-table idiom, so each closed set of words has ONE home and the enum an author meets
is `or()` of that table's derived word list:

```
_method  (22 words) -> _methodWord -> #CdpInput.method
_action  ( 3 words) -> _actionWord -> #CdpInput.action
```

That is the 2 `union` rows `design/dpat-baseline.tsv` records for
`plugin-cdp/candy/plugin-cdp/schema/cdp.cue` — two disjunctions, one row each, both pure word
lists. No other row names this repo, so this PR retires the whole of its census debt (2 of 2;
0 left).

**How this repo was picked.** It is the third unit of this batch, from the
entirely-word-list set re-derived from `git show origin/main:design/dpat-baseline.tsv` —
strip a trailing `@go(...)` and enclosing parens, drop an empty trailing alternative, then ask
whether every alternative is a quoted string. Ranked by row count with ties broken by word
count, this batch's three are `plugin-appium` (2 rows, 51 words — merged as
opencharly/plugin-appium#12), `plugin-vm` (2 rows, 30 words — opencharly/plugin-vm#94) and
`plugin-cdp` (2 rows, 25 words — THIS PR).

**This one's shape is the plain one, and that is worth stating because the siblings were not:
`method` is written WITHOUT enclosing parens and WITHOUT a trailing `@go(...)`.** The other two
units of this batch carried both, so a sweep script anchored on `(` + `@go(Method,type=string)`
would find nothing here — the extraction asserts it found exactly 22 quoted alternatives and
aborts otherwise, rather than writing an empty table.

The two sets stay two tables because they are two vocabularies: `method` is the CDP method
catalog the dispatcher switches on, `action` is the DETACHED screencast recorder's bracket
(`start|stop|status`, session_method.go's `switch in.Action`). Each is pinned against its OWN
router, in both directions.

No word moves: the accepted sets are IDENTICAL before and after — 22 methods, 3 actions — read
from the compiled CUE on both sides (192-case differential below). The umbrella leg — the pin
plus the emission-diffed census — is the lead's, by emission; this PR touches neither
`design/dpat-baseline.tsv` nor the umbrella.

opencharly/plugin-appium#12 and opencharly/plugin-vm#94 (this batch's first two units),
